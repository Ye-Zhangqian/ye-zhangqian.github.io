YE Zhangqian is currently a 2024 Ph.D. student in Landscape Architecture at the Department of Landscape Architecture, School of Architecture, Tsinghua University. His research interests focus on national parks and protected areas, public health, climate change, and landscape planning and design. He has published first-authored papers in leading Chinese and international journals, including Landscape and Urban Planning and Chinese Landscape Architecture, and has contributed as a major author to publications in prominent journals such as Journal of Environmental Management and Biological Conservation. His first-authored work has been publicly featured by Tsinghua University’s official Facebook, LinkedIn, and X accounts, as well as by the official WeChat accounts of the School of Architecture, Tsinghua University, and the Institute for National Parks, National Forestry and Grassland Administration–Tsinghua University.
   
During his studies at Tsinghua University, he has received multiple honors and awards, including the inaugural Tsinghua University “Three Goods” Student Award, the Comprehensive First-Class Scholarship for Graduate Students at Tsinghua University, the Comprehensive Excellence Scholarship for Undergraduate Students at Tsinghua University, the Outstanding Student Leader Award, the Outstanding Communist Youth League Member Award, and the National Inspirational Scholarship. His proposed climate change solution won second place in the inaugural Asian Youth Student Innovation Competition on Climate Change and Health, and his team members were invited to deliver oral presentations at the 30th United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP30) in Belém, Brazil, and the Lancet Countdown Asia Conference on Climate Change and Health in Beijing.
   
YE Zhangqian has previously served as Secretary of the Youth League Committee of the School of Architecture, Tsinghua University; President of the Student Union of the School of Architecture; and Event Services Assistant for the Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics. He currently serves as a “double-shouldered” student political counselor at Tsinghua University.
