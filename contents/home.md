
本人现为清华大学建筑学院景观学系风景园林专业2024级博士研究生，研究方向聚焦国家公园与自然保护地、公共健康、气候变化及景观规划设计。目前以第一作者在《Landscape and Urban Planning》《中国园林》等国内外重要期刊发表论文。曾获清华大学研究生综合一等奖学金、优秀学生干部、国家励志奖学金等荣誉，曾经担任建筑学院学生会主席、2022北京冬奥会赛事服务助理等，目前担任建筑学院团委书记、“双肩挑”学生政治辅导员等职务，兼具学术研究与组织管理能力。  
I am currently a 2024 Ph.D. candidate in Landscape Architecture at the Department of Landscape Architecture, School of Architecture, Tsinghua University. My research focuses on national parks and protected areas, public health, climate change, and landscape planning and design. To date, I have published papers as first author in leading journals including Landscape and Urban Planning and Chinese Landscape Architecture. I have received multiple honors, including the Comprehensive First-Class Scholarship for Graduate Students at Tsinghua University, the Outstanding Student Leader Award, and the National Inspirational Scholarship. I previously served as President of the Student Union of the School of Architecture and as an Event Services Assistant for the Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics. I currently serve as Secretary of the Youth League Committee of the School of Architecture and as a student counselor at Tsinghua University, demonstrating both strong academic research capacity and organizational leadership.
